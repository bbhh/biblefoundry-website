## How it works

Open the app and swipe through that day's five Psalms (and a chapter of Proverbs). Let every word or phrase or verse move you to praise, confession, thanksgiving, and supplication before God. Then come back the next day for another set of Psalms and Proverbs.

## Release notes

### Five Psalms v3 coming soon on Android and iOS

**November 20, 2026**

Version 3 is another complete rewrite of Five Psalms from scratch, but now with separate native apps for Android and for iOS. That means each app is snappier and looks like a native app (because it is!). And two brand new features that you'll love -- you can highlight your favorite portions of the text, and you can schedule a daily reminder to open the app.

This version is currently in internal beta testing for Android, and in final polishing for iOS. Stay tuned, they will be available for download soon!

**April 29, 2024**

Version 2 is a completely new rewrite of Five Psalms from the ground up, which also features a new date picker as well as a new Spanish Bible version (Reina Valera Actualizada (RVA)).
